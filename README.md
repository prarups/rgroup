## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Interactive 3D Experiences (Three.js & WebGL)**:
   - **Hero 3D Canvas**: Interactive constellation of digital leads & rotating particle matrix reacting in real-time to mouse movement and scroll depth.
   - **Growth System 3D Canvas**: 5-node glowing geometric pipeline demonstrating the `TARGET → ADVERTISE → GENERATE → FOLLOW UP → GROW` flow.

2. **Full Multi-Page Architecture**:
   - `Home` (`/` or `#home`): Problem-Solution comparison, 3D Hero, 7 core services preview, 5-step growth flow, guarantee badge, founder credentials.
   - `Services` (`#services`): Deep dive into all 7 core offerings (Paid Social Ads, Targeted Lead Gen, WhatsApp Daily Leads, Ad Shoots, Poster Designs, Growth Consulting, Strategy Consulting) with checklists & direct WhatsApp triggers.
   - `Growth System` (`#growth-system`): Complete 5-stage systematic breakdown with 3D pipeline visualization.
   - `About Founder` (`#about`): Profile of A. Raghul, 4+ years track record, 100+ clients milestone, and R GROUP corporate backing.
   - `Contact` (`#contact`): Lead qualification form, budget slider, operating hours, direct phone, and FAQs.
   - `Guarantee Terms` (`#guarantee`): "NO LEADS? MONEY-BACK GUARANTEE*" official policy documentation for complete transparency and Meta Ads compliance.

3. **Instant WhatsApp Lead Generation**:
   - Every CTA automatically formats user responses into pre-filled WhatsApp messages for instant closing.
   - Interactive Lead Inquiry Modal with business qualification fields.
   - Floating pulse WhatsApp widget.

4. **SEO & Performance**:
   - Complete OpenGraph, Twitter Cards, and Meta tags.
   - Schema.org `ProfessionalService` JSON-LD structured data.
   - Top neon scroll progress bar tracking page read progress.
